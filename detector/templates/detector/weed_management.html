Understanding Weeds

A weed is any plant growing where it is not wanted. Weeds compete with crops for light, water, nutrients and space, and can also shelter pests and diseases.

🌿

Broadleaf Weeds

Wide leaves with net-like veins. They can often be distinguished from narrow-leaved crops such as cereals.

Wide leaves
🌾

Grass Weeds

Narrow, blade-like leaves with parallel veins. Young grass weeds can look very similar to cereal crops.

Narrow leaves
🌱

Sedges

Grass-like plants that often have triangular stems. They are common in wet or poorly drained soils.

Often found in wet soil

Remember: These are general characteristics. Confirm plant identity before removing it, especially when weeds resemble the crop.

Critical Period for Weed Control

Crops are often most sensitive to weed competition during early growth. Controlling weeds early can protect yield better than waiting until late in the season. The exact period depends on the crop and growing conditions.

Practical field guidance

🛠️ Weed Control Methods

Different situations call for different approaches. Integrated weed management combines suitable methods rather than relying on just one.

🧑‍🌾

Manual Removal

Hand pulling or hoeing weeds that have been correctly identified.

  • Best for small plots, gardens and young weeds.
  • Remove as much of the root as practical to reduce regrowth.
  • Weed after rain or irrigation, when the soil is soft.
  • Remove weeds before they flower and set seed.
  • Place removed weeds where they cannot re-root or spread seed.

✅ Pros: Low cost and precise; no herbicide needed.

⚠️ Cons: Labour-intensive and slow on large fields.

Plan through the season

📅 Weed Management Calendar

These stages are general. Exact timing depends on the crop, region and season.

🌱
BEFORE SOWING

Prepare the Field

  • Prepare a clean seedbed and remove existing weeds.
  • Use certified, weed-free seed.
  • Clean bunds, irrigation channels and field borders.
🌿
EARLY CROP GROWTH

Monitor and Act Early

  • This is often the most important time to manage competition.
  • Scout every few days and remove small weeds early.
  • Use inter-row tools once crop rows are established.
🌾
MID SEASON

Keep Monitoring

  • Look for new weed growth after rain or irrigation.
  • Target affected patches where appropriate.
  • Avoid damaging crop roots during control.
🌻
BEFORE HARVEST

Prevent Seed Spread

  • Remove weeds that are about to set seed when practical.
  • Clean harvesting machines to avoid spreading weed seeds.
🍂
AFTER HARVEST

Prepare for Next Season

  • Record where weeds were most troublesome.
  • Plan suitable crop rotation or cover crops.
Responsible weed management

✅ Do's and Don'ts

✅ Do

  • Walk the field and confirm each plant before removing it.
  • Control weeds early, before they flower.
  • Combine more than one suitable control method.
  • Keep records of detections and management actions.
  • Clean tools and machines between fields.

❌ Don't

  • Remove a plant based only on an AI prediction.
  • Allow weeds to set seed when they can be safely controlled.
  • Use a herbicide not approved for your crop and situation.
  • Exceed the product label dose or ignore safety directions.
  • Spray in strong wind or before heavy rain.
